# JournalWeb Privacy Policy

_Effective 4 November 2026_

## In short

JournalWeb is made by Noxonic Software MB. Your journal stays on your device. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data, and the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## What the app stores

Your entries, concepts, connections, journals, name and settings are saved in a database on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos you attach to entries are copied from your photo library into the app's own storage on your device, and are not sent anywhere either. The app only sees the photos you pick.

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device. No push service or server is involved.

The home screen widget only opens a new entry. It shows nothing from your journal and sends nothing anywhere.

Uninstalling the app, or using "Clear current journal" in Settings, deletes that data. Your device's own backups (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include it, under that provider's terms. On Android, Google's backup includes your entries but not your photos.

## Purchases

JournalWeb Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. The store handles the payment and its details. The app only keeps a record on your device that Pro is unlocked, so it can restore it later. Apple's and Google's privacy policies apply to the purchase.

## App lock

If you turn on App lock, your device checks your Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. The app only learns whether the check passed; it never sees or stores your biometric data.

## Export

Export creates a file of your journals and opens your device's share sheet. Where that file goes is up to you, and the app you send it to handles it under its own terms. Import reads an export file you pick and adds its journals on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Noxonic Software MB is the data controller for JournalWeb. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete or hand over. Your journal is on your device and fully in your control: you can edit or delete anything in the app, and Export gives you a complete copy in JSON.

Apple and Google process your purchase as independent controllers. Requests about that data go to them.

You can still contact us with any privacy question, and you have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app, with a new effective date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the developer email on the JournalWeb App Store or Google Play listing.
